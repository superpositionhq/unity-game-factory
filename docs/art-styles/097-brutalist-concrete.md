## Visual definition

- **Shape language and proportions:** Massive raw-concrete volumes: deep cantilevers, heavy overhanging upper floors, thick columns (pilotis), repetitive deep-set window grids, bush-hammered ribbed walls, monumental stairs and voids. Simple geometry at very large scale.
- **Line and outline:** No outline.
- **Shading model:** Smooth, matte, soft-shadowed. Form reads through large planes of light and shade.
- **Palette:** `#8c8a84` `#6b6964` `#a9a69f` `#4a4946` `#c4c1b8` `#2e2d2b`. Concrete grey from pale to charcoal; almost no colour. Optional single muted accent (a rust door, a red carpet).
- **Lighting:** Overcast daylight or shafts from high slot windows and skylights; long gradients on big walls.
- **Texture and surface:** Board-marked concrete (imprint of timber formwork planks), tie-rod holes in a regular grid, pour lines, rain streaks, ribbed bush-hammered surfaces.
- **Atmosphere and post-processing:** Subtle ambient occlusion and slight haze in large interiors.
- **Camera and framing:** Wide, low, looking up at overhanging masses, or symmetric views of atria.
- **UI and typography:** Plain flat panels in concrete grey with square corners and no ornament, set on a strict grid; icons are bold wayfinding pictograms. Type is a heavy grotesque sans in large capitals, like lettering cast into a building wall, in pale `#c4c1b8` or charcoal `#2e2d2b`. Counters are big plain numerals, with one muted rust or red accent for alerts.
- **VFX and motion:** Concrete dust, falling chips and grey debris chunks; hits knock out puffs of powder and leave pale chipped marks. Motion is heavy, with dust drifting slowly through light shafts and large masses that move with inertia.

## Unity recipe

### Assets

Massive raw-concrete volumes: deep cantilevers, heavy overhanging upper floors, thick columns, repetitive deep-set window grids, ribbed walls, monumental stairs and voids. Build with ProBuilder's API at very large scale; simple geometry, few pieces. Texture is specific and should be generated by script, tiled in world units: board-marked concrete (horizontal plank imprints 15 to 20 cm high with wood grain in the normal map), tie-rod holes on a regular grid, pour lines every few metres, vertical rain streaks below ledges, and a ribbed bush-hammered variant. Keep surfaces intact and clean-cut: no rubble or battle damage.

### Materials

`Universal Render Pipeline/Lit`: albedo in the grey range `#6b6964` to `#c4c1b8`, metallic 0, smoothness 0.1 to 0.2, with the board-mark normal map at moderate strength and an occlusion map for holes and joints. Use world-space or triplanar UVs in a small custom variant (calling `UniversalFragmentPBR`) so the plank scale stays constant on huge walls without stretching. Rain streaks blend in by a mask driven by world height below ledges. One optional accent material, a rust door or red carpet. Glass is dark and recessed.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Overcast daylight: a weak directional light with very soft shadows and a strong grey sky ambient, so big walls carry long gradients. Bake lightmaps and Adaptive Probe Volumes with sky occlusion; bounce light between concrete planes defines the forms. Interiors use shafts from high slot windows and skylights: spot or directional light through real openings, with faint additive shaft meshes. Thin grey haze (exponential fog, low density) in large interiors. SSAO on, subtle, large radius. An overcast HDRI or flat grey sky.

### Post-processing

Neutral tonemapping, saturation -30 except the accent. Slight contrast lift. No Bloom, grain or vignette. TAA or MSAA 4x. Frame with wide lenses and human figures small against the mass.

### UI and effects

Plain flat concrete-grey panels with square corners, no ornament, on a strict grid; bold wayfinding pictograms. Font asset from a heavy grotesque in large capitals such as Archivo Black or Anton, in `#c4c1b8` or `#2e2d2b`, like lettering cast into a wall; big plain numerals, one muted rust or red for alerts. Effects: concrete dust puffs (lit, grey, slow), falling chips and debris chunks as mesh particles, pale chipped decals where hits land, dust drifting slowly through light shafts. Large masses move with long acceleration and deceleration.

## Do / Do not

Do:

- Build massive cantilevered concrete volumes.
- Show board marks and a tie-hole grid.
- Keep the image nearly monochrome grey.
- Light softly with large gradients.

Do not:

- Do not paint surfaces white with coloured accents; that reads as `035`.
- Do not smash the concrete into rubble with grit and scratches; that reads as `030`.
- Do not use smooth white moulded panels; that reads as `091`.
- Do not add gold, marble and ornament; that reads as `096`.

## Nearest styles

- `030` Gritty desaturated war realism: this style shows intact, clean-cut concrete masses in soft grey daylight; `030` is darker, with rubble, grit, scratches and battle damage on everything.
- `035` White city with primary accents: walls here are mid-grey with visible board marks and pour lines; `035` is clean painted white with bright primary accents.
- `091` Clinical white space-age: this style is grey, rough-cast and heavy; `091` is bright white with smooth moulded panels and glowing light strips.
