## Visual definition

- **Shape language and proportions:** Victorian machinery: exposed gears, flywheels, pistons, boilers with domed ends, pressure gauges, valve wheels, riveted plates, copper pipe runs, leather straps and wood panelling. Ornate cast brackets on functional machines.
- **Line and outline:** No outline.
- **Shading model:** Physically based and smooth, dominated by warm metallic reflections.
- **Palette:** `#b5802e` `#7a4a1e` `#3a2a1c` `#c9a36a` `#5a6b5e` `#2f1f14` `#e6c88a` `#8a2f1f`. Brass, copper and bronze with dark wood, oxblood leather, iron black and verdigris green.
- **Lighting:** Warm gaslight and furnace glow, shafts of light through steam; dark surroundings.
- **Texture and surface:** Polished and tarnished metal, rivet rows, wood grain, stitched leather, soot, verdigris in crevices, engraved dials.
- **Atmosphere and post-processing:** Steam, warm haze, bloom on lamps and furnace, slight vignette.
- **Camera and framing:** Medium shots of machinery with shallow depth; low angles on large engines.
- **UI and typography:** Brass-framed panels with riveted corners, round pressure-gauge dials and engraved plates on dark wood or oxblood leather; icons look like etched or cast metal emblems. Type is an ornate Victorian serif with engraved slab capitals in cream `#e6c88a`. Counters are needle gauges or mechanical digit wheels like an odometer.
- **VFX and motion:** Steam jets, furnace sparks, soot puffs and spinning gears; hits throw orange sparks and bursts of white steam. Motion is mechanical and weighty: pistons pump in a steady rhythm, flywheels spin up slowly, and machines shudder when they start.

## Unity recipe

### Assets

Victorian machinery: exposed gears, flywheels, pistons, domed boilers, pressure gauges, valve wheels, riveted plates, copper pipe runs, leather straps, wood panelling, ornate cast brackets. Steampunk and industrial packs fit; keep full PBR maps. Gears, pipes and rivet rows are also quick to generate by script. Enrich in an editor step: verdigris in crevices from the occlusion map (green tint, lower metallic, lower smoothness), soot near furnaces, tarnish variation in smoothness. Engrave dial faces as textures generated by script.

### Materials

`Universal Render Pipeline/Lit`, metallic workflow. Brass: albedo `#b5802e` to `#c9a36a`, metallic 1, smoothness 0.6 to 0.85 varied by a tarnish mask. Copper slightly redder; iron dark with low smoothness. Wood and oxblood leather are non-metal with normal maps for grain and stitching. Verdigris `#5a6b5e` appears through the mask. Furnace mouths and lamp mantles use `_EMISSION` in orange. Glass gauge covers use transparent Lit. No custom shader is required; the look depends on correct metallic values and on reflections.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Warm gaslight and furnace glow in dark surroundings: warm point and spot lights (2200 to 2800 K) with soft shadows, flickering slightly by script; furnace lights in deep orange. Metal needs things to reflect: place baked reflection probes with box projection in every room and include emissive lamps in the bake. Bake Adaptive Probe Volumes for warm bounce. Warm brown exponential fog and steam particles; light shafts through steam as additive cone meshes. SSAO on, medium.

### Post-processing

ACES tonemapping. Bloom on lamps and furnace (threshold 1, moderate intensity, warm tint). Slight Vignette. White Balance warm, Split Toning with warm highlights and brown shadows. No heavy desaturation: the metal colour must stay rich. TAA, HDR on.

### UI and effects

Brass-framed panels with riveted corners on dark wood or oxblood leather, round pressure-gauge dials and engraved plates; icons as etched or cast metal emblems, rendered from models. Font asset from an ornate Victorian serif with slab capitals such as Rye, Vast Shadow or Playfair Display SC, cream `#e6c88a`. Counters are needle gauges (rotate a needle image) or digit wheels that scroll vertically like an odometer. Effects: steam jets from lit particles with fast initial speed, furnace sparks, soot puffs, spinning gears; hits throw orange sparks and white steam. Machines shudder on start-up and pistons keep a steady rhythm.

## Do / Do not

Do:

- Make brass and copper the dominant materials.
- Show gears, pistons, gauges and rivets.
- Light with warm lamps and furnace glow.
- Provide a warm environment map for reflections.

Do not:

- Do not use cold grey industrial panels; that reads as `092`.
- Do not use sleek stepped geometric ornament in gold and black; that reads as `096`.
- Do not soften into low-saturation painted greys; that reads as `026`.
- Do not use chrome and pastel plastics; that reads as `099`.

## Nearest styles

- `096` Art Deco streamline: surfaces here are busy with rivets, gears, tarnish and soot in many warm metal and wood tones; `096` is smooth, polished and sparing, in black, gold and teal with clean geometric inlay.
- `026` Oily concept-art realism: this style has rich, warm brass and copper colour with crisp material detail; `026` is cold and greyed, with loose painted surfaces.
- `092` Industrial used-future: this style is warm metal glowing under gaslight; `092` is grey-green painted metal in dimmer, colder light.
